Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

KitchenAI helps a household keep one pantry and shopping list and get recipe ideas from what it has. This page says what the app stores, what it sends and to whom, and how to delete it. Contact: CONTACT_EMAIL_PLACEHOLDER.

What is stored, and where

  • Your account: an identifier, and, if you sign in with Google or Apple, the email and name those services give the app. You can also use the app as a guest, with no email at all.
  • Your profile: the name you choose to show your kitchen, your food preferences and things you avoid (for example an allergy or a diet), and the app's language.
  • Your kitchen: its members and their chosen names, the pantry (ingredients, amounts, where they are kept, expiry dates), shopping lists, and saved recipes. Everyone in the kitchen sees this, and nobody else.
  • On your device only: the last recipe ideas, appearance and reminder settings. Reminders are notifications your phone schedules itself; nothing about them leaves the device.

Everything except what stays on the device is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), on Google Cloud servers.

What is sent to make recipe ideas

When you ask for ideas, the app sends your food preferences and avoids, those shared by your kitchen's members, your pantry's ingredients and amounts (including the names you typed for items not in our list), the number of servings, the filters you chose and the app's language. It sends no name, email or account identifier. A function in our Google Cloud project passes this to Google's Gemini model through Vertex AI, under Google Cloud's terms, and returns the ideas. We count requests per account per day to keep the service within its budget.

Checking that requests come from the app

The app uses Firebase App Check, which asks Google Play Integrity (Android) or Apple App Attest and DeviceCheck (iOS) to confirm a request comes from a genuine copy of the app. Those services receive information about the device and app installation, not your content.

Crash reports

When the app crashes or a recipe request fails, it sends a report to Firebase Crashlytics: the technical error, the device model, the operating system and app versions, and a random installation identifier. It contains no name, email, account identifier, ingredient, recipe or location.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no selling or sharing of data with advertisers, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No analytics on how you use the app.
  • Your data is not used to train any model by us.

Who can see it

The members of your kitchen, you, and Google as the provider that stores and processes it for us. We do not give it to anyone else unless the law requires it.

How long it is kept

As long as your account exists. Daily request counters expire on their own. When you leave a kitchen you stop seeing its data; the remaining members keep it.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile › Delete account. It deletes your profile and account, and every kitchen you were the only member of, with its pantry, lists and recipes. A kitchen you shared stays with the other members, and if you owned it, ownership passes to another member. It cannot be undone. Without the app, see Delete your account.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how it is used, by writing to the contact above. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

KitchenAI is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too, and a change that matters is announced in the app.
