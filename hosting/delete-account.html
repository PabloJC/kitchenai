Delete your KitchenAI account

From the app

  1. Open KitchenAI and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Without the app

Write to CONTACT_EMAIL_PLACEHOLDER from the email you signed in with, or, if you used the app as a guest, say so and give the name your kitchen shows for you. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account, your profile and your food preferences.
  • Every kitchen you were the only member of, with its pantry, shopping lists and saved recipes.

What is kept

  • A kitchen you shared stays with its other members, without you. If you owned it, another member becomes the owner.
  • Daily request counters, which expire on their own.

Deleting cannot be undone. See the privacy policy for everything the app stores.
